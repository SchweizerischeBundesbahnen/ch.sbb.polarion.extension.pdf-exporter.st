What a refused external resource costs the export

A stylesheet keeps its declarations. The rule which paints this box also names an address the policy refuses. The box has to keep its yellow fill, its blue border and this text. Only the background picture is lost, and a page which lost the border or the fill says the whole stylesheet was dropped.

A picture the document carries stands. It travels in the file itself, so no policy applies to it.

A picture from a refused address becomes a transparent placeholder. The frame beside this text is empty, and the text around it stands where it stood.
